📚 CYBER BARONS HANDBOOK
how everything works here — from a cutlet to a Mega-Clan
🪙 Coins: the ecosystem's main currency

Coins are the in-game currency of Cyber Barons. You spend them to create teams, play duels, bid at auctions and show off your wealth in the TOP-100.

How to earn

  • 🎁 +500 🪙 right when you sign up;
  • 🚜 the farm mines coins around the clock, even while you sleep;
  • 🕹️ wins in games against the bot: +2 🪙 per win (Cyber-Safe and Code breaker — +5, Memory — +3);
  • 🧠 intelligence levels in the quiz: +100, +200, +300… 🪙 per level;
  • ⚔️ duels with live players — take your opponent's bank;
  • 🤝 +500 🪙, a 🥩 cutlet and 10 of each basic resource (⛏ 💧 💎 ⚙️) for every active friend who joins through your referral link;
  • 🛒 coin packs in the shop for Telegram Stars.

The economy is protected from inflation: losses, duel and market fees, and winning auction bids burn coins for good. Over time, your wealth only grows more valuable.

🚜 Cyber-farm: robots and cutlets

🤖 Intern Bot — 1 🪙/hour, free and forever

Every player's first robot. Activate it on the farm screen (the “Activate your Intern Bot” button) — and it starts mining 1 🪙 an hour forever: no fuel, no service life. At intelligence levels 10, 20, 30, 40, 50… in the quiz another Intern Bot is waiting for you — claim it with the “🎁 Claim your Intern Bot” button on the farm, in the quiz round summary or in the app. Boosts work on it too: with Nuclear Boost x5 an Intern Bot gives 5 🪙/hour.

♾ Eternal Miner. An Intern Bot can be promoted to a Miner Bot forever (5 🪙/hour with no expiry) — a shop item for 999 ★, with one condition: at least 3 active friends who joined through your link (what “active” means — see the referral section). This way the eternal robot goes to those who grow the ecosystem.

🤖 Miner Bot — 5 🪙/hour

A level-one robot. Works on its own, needs no fuel, grinds with no days off.

⚡ Cyber-Driller — 10 🪙/hour

A level-two robot, twice as powerful but hungry: EVERY Driller eats its own 1 cyber-cutlet per 24 hours of work. One cutlet feeds only one robot: keep 3 Drillers — have 3 cutlets a day ready, 10 Drillers — 10 cutlets. A whole cutlet is taken from the Fridge when the daily shift starts. Not enough cutlets for everyone — hungry Drillers stop until they're fed.

🥩 Cyber-cutlets, storage and the Fridge

Cutlets are kept in storage (purchases and bonuses land there), but robots eat from the Fridge. Move cutlets over with the “Refuel” buttons on the farm screen. The Fridge can be upgraded in the shop: 10 / 50 / 100 cutlets.

🎁 Every 24 hours tap “CLAIM BONUS” on the farm in the app or in the bot chat — a free cutlet for your storage!

🔥 Robot service life

A purchased robot lives 30 days from the moment of purchase, then burns out — buy a new one in the shop (the “For the farm” section). The date of the next burnout is shown on the farm screen. The exceptions are Intern Bots and the Eternal Miner: they never burn out.

🛸 Master Drone

A legendary helper for 365 days: while the drone is on the farm, robot wear stops completely — a robot's 30 days of life only tick down while the drone is inactive. A rock-solid investment for a big farm.

⚡ Boosts (they stack!)

  • 🔋 Nuclear Boost x5 — mining of all robots ×5 for 30 days. The price of power: a Driller's cutlet burns three times faster — 1 cutlet per 8 hours instead of a day. The boost works with any number of cutlets, even one; if the boost ends while a cutlet is still burning, the rest is used at the normal pace — nothing is lost;
  • ❄️ Nitrogen Cooling +40% — Miner 5→7, Driller 10→14 🪙/hour, cutlet use doesn't go up;
  • together: Miner 35, Driller 70 🪙/hour. A monster farm!
🧠 Quiz and intelligence levels

The quiz is a brain sprint: 30 seconds per answer, run out of time and it's a loss. Correct answer: +2 🪙, mistake: −1. Questions come in rounds of 10, a 50/50 hint costs 10 🪙, and every 10 correct answers give you 🪨 stone.

🏅 Intelligence levels

Each level is a series of correct answers, and the counter for each series starts from zero:

  • level 1: 10 correct → reward +100 🪙;
  • level 2: another 20 correct → +200 🪙;
  • level 3: another 30 → +300… and so on forever — level 100 brings +10,000 🪙!

Your 🧠 level shows in your profile, in the teammates list and in the TOP-100 — a public badge of intelligence in the ecosystem.

Quiz limit — 30 wins/day, resets at 00:00 UTC. Want to level up faster? The shop has limit removal for 24 hours or 30 days.

🕹️ Quick games against the bot

Three classic games: a win +2 🪙, a loss −1, a draw takes nothing and gives nothing. Each one's limit — 15 wins/day (resets at 00:00 UTC).

  • 🪨 RPS — rock, paper, scissors against the bot;
  • 📈 Higher-Lower — guess which way the hidden number will go;
  • 🔐 Cyber-Safe — pick the code out of 4 options: crack it — jackpot +5 🪙, miss — the alarm goes off and −1.

⚡ Unlimited from the shop lifts the limits in all games at once.

🧩 New games, the Daily case and quests
  • ➗ Math — 20 seconds per problem, 30 wins/day; every 10 correct — ⚡ electricity;
  • 🏳️ Flags and capitals — 20 seconds per answer, 30 wins/day, resource — 💎 crystals;
  • 💻 Code breaker — guess 4 different digits in 10 tries: +5 🪙 and ⚙️ 2 parts, 10 wins/day;
  • 🃏 Memory — find 6 pairs in no more than 12 moves: +3 🪙 and 💧 hydrogen, 15 wins/day.

🕵️ Daily case — one case for everyone per day (UTC): 4 suspects, clues open one by one, and you can accuse only once. The fewer clues you needed, the bigger the reward: 40 / 30 / 20 / 10 🪙, and every 7th day of a solved-case streak gives another +50 🪙.

🗺 Daily quests — every day everyone gets the same 10 quests. Without Unlimited, 3 new completions count per day, and completed ones can be replayed as much as you like. For the first completion — 20–50 🪙 and 🪨 stone; ⭐ epics (20+ minutes) are open only with ⚡ Unlimited and give 100 🪙 and 🪨 5.

⚔️ Duels: player vs player

An RPS duel with a live player for a stake. Three tables, like in poker — pick what fits your pocket: 10 🪙 100 🪙 1000 🪙.

Bank rules

  • your stake goes into the bank as soon as you sit down at the table;
  • the winner takes 80% of the bank (stake ×1.6), 20% burns for good — duels drive deflation;
  • with ⚡ Unlimited the winner's fee is half as much — 10% (winnings ×1.8);
  • duels are coins only: resources are mined in other games;
  • a draw — an instant replay of gestures, the bank stays on the table;
  • you get 60 seconds per gesture; if you stay silent, a random generator picks for you;
  • no opponent found within 30 minutes — your stake comes back automatically;
  • ⚔️ challenge a friend by link — a private table that skips the queue, the link lasts 24 hours; a newcomer who joins through it becomes your referral.

Duel wins count toward your team's score in battles with a duel nomination — so train up!

🏆 Teams and clans

A team is your crew of up to 50 fighters. Creating one costs 100,000 🪙: come up with a name, a motto and an emblem. You can start with a squad: up to 5 fighters for 7,500 🪙, and later the captain can expand it into a team for 92,500 🪙 (the same price in total). In the shop a team can be upgraded to a Mega-Clan with 1000 seats. All of this is in the “🏆 Team” tab.

👥 Teammates list

The “🏆 Team” tab has the full roster: every fighter with their 🧠 intelligence level and a clickable @username — message each other, set up your own chats and get ready for battles. The list is visible only to members of this team — no outsiders.

👑 Captaincy

If the captain leaves the crew, the crown passes to the most loyal veteran — the one who joined earliest. A team with no fighters left disbands automatically.

Why join? Team battles, a shared score, prizes for every fighter of the winning teams — solo players don't get any of that.

🏟️ Team and clan battles — the main events

Battles are not a daily routine but events, like a world cup: the administration announces a battle in the game (passport → “📣 Events”), and it has a name, dates and a nomination.

How it works

  • the nomination is what gets measured: mine the most, win the most duels or quiz rounds, bring the most active newcomers, and more;
  • what counts is the COMBINED result of all players on the team — every fighter brings victory closer, so invite the most active players to your crew!
  • scoring happens strictly within the battle window — boosting your score after the fact is impossible;
  • team points and prizes go to fighters who have been on the team for at least 24 hours by the end of the battle: jumping to the leaders a minute before the finish won't work (the same goes for team expeditions and mine income);
  • there are team battles, clan battles, teams and clans together — and personal events: for example, whose reel on Instagram, YouTube Shorts or TikTok takes off best, or who brings in the most active friends;
  • announcements are in the passport (“📣 Events”), ongoing battles and your place — in the TOP tab → “🎯 Events”; the bot sends a private message to everyone who gets a reward, saying what was credited.

⛏ Mine battles and the 🔋 entry

The organizer can put several mines up at once: as many mines as there are prize places (1st place gets the most valuable one). Only teams and clans that have submitted a full battery entry fight for mines: a team — 5 🔋, a clan — 25 🔋. Any fighter can chip in on the “⛏ Mines” tab — pool them as a team!

🎁 Prizes

Prizes and the number of prize places for each battle are set by the administration. Prizes are in-game only: coins, resources, crafted items, robots and boosts for various periods, suit parts and artifacts. In team battles every fighter of a winning team gets the reward.

🗺 World map: solo and squad expeditions

The “🗺 Map” tab in the game app is a world map where the administration posts expedition targets from time to time (up to 10 at once): solo, team and clan ones. Each has its own story, its own prizes and its own results hour; results can come earlier if a set number of players reach the target.

How an expedition goes

  • you set out from your side of the map and slowly walk to the target in plain sight — the marker moves along with time;
  • the target shows how long the trip takes and whether you'll make it by the results hour: if you won't, you can't set out, and anyone who's late because of forks doesn't take part in the results;
  • gear: solo — 💧 10 hydrogen, a team — 🔋 5 batteries, a Mega-Clan — 🔋 25 and 🥩 10 cutlets. Fighters chip in one at a time; with the full set the squad sets out, and if it isn't collected by the results or the target is cancelled, everything comes back;
  • a solo trip takes 10–30 minutes, a squad's takes longer; at any time — one solo expedition per player and one squad per team.

🔀 Forks

Along the way you hit forks: a storm, an abandoned warehouse, a rival squad, a rope bridge… Each has 2–3 options with their own consequences: more cargo, a risk of losses or a shortcut. The nearest fork is visible right away, with a “to the fork” timer — pick an option in advance and it kicks in when you get there (you can change your mind until then). Didn't pick? After reaching the fork you have time until the next one; staying silent means the squad walks on by: no loot, no losses and no delay. In a team squad the vote decides: if everyone has voted, it kicks in right away, otherwise the majority wins.

🔎 Scouting forks

You can learn an option's consequences in advance with the “Scout this fork” button. An expedition gets as many scouts as the squad leader has (solo — you, in a team — the captain) 🛰 Spy drones and 🔭 Scouting levels — whichever is lower: 1 spy and Scouting 1 — one fork, 5 and 5 — all five. The first expedition of every season is blind for everyone.

🏁 Results

At the results hour, places are ranked by cargo among those who made it. How many prize places there are and what each gets is up to the organizer; the others who made it may get a participation reward. The bot sends a private message to everyone who gets a reward, saying what was credited.

📦 What cargo is

Cargo is your expedition score. Everyone starts with 100, forks add (loot) or take away (losses) — places in the results go by cargo.

🤖 Combat drones

Three kinds of personal companions, an exclusive achievement: building one is as hard as the Shuttle, or buy one in the workshop for 100,000 🪙 (the coins burn). There's no limit on how many, their effects add up, drones are never used up and don't take storage space.

  • 🛡 Guard drone — raiders carry off less from you; on expeditions you lose less cargo;
  • ⚔️ Attack drone — your raids bring in more; on expeditions more loot at combat forks;
  • 🛰 Spy drone — more targets in raid scouting; on expeditions a shorter trip, and together with 🔭 Scouting — fork scouting.

Exact percentages are on the drone's card in the workshop.

🧍 Character: suits and artifacts

The “🧍 Character” tab right after the passport: an avatar, 5 slots (helmet, jacket, gloves, trousers, boots) and a bag. Only a full set of one suit gives a bonus: ⚡ Electrician — electricity drops more often for wins, ⛏ Miner — once a day a percentage of the farm's mining, 🪖 Soldier — more loot and spoils, faster expeditions, 🥷 Stealth — a chance to stay out of raiders' scouting, 🔬 Scientist — a shorter break between technology levels, 🔧 Engineer — cheaper workshop recipes.

Each part has three levels: Basic, Improved and Legendary. Upgrading needs resources, coins and part XP — events the part has lived through while worn; Legendary also needs a Lab technology. The bonus is locked in at the moment of the action: take the suit off and what you've already got stays. Parts drop in events (free — up to 5 per season), and Basic ones are sold on the market right in the tab.

💠 Artifacts — three belt slots under the avatar: Storm heart, Stone of plenty, Crimson sunset, Phantom shade, Insight orb and Mechanical warrior. Each one boosts its own bonus line and stacks with the suit. One per season is free, the rest are on the market.

🔨 Auctions and VIP ID

From time to time the administration puts unique lots up for auction — for example, legendary VIP IDs: BARON-777.

Fair refundable bids

Your bid is held right away, but if someone outbids you, the whole amount comes back instantly. Only the winner pays when the lot closes.

💎 What a VIP ID gives

It's your personal ID in the ecosystem: it shows off in your profile, appears in the TOP-100, and transfers work with it — friends can send coins straight to BARON-777 instead of boring digits.

📈 Market: cutlets and resources between players

On the market players trade cutlets, all resources and even the ☢️ Core for coins. The more deals, the more coins burn in fees — this keeps the coin's value up. The full market is the “📈 Market” tab in the game app: price index, a 7-day chart, who's selling, who's buying, your orders and resource swaps.

  • sell order: the item goes into an order and waits for a buyer; you can withdraw it at any time;
  • buy bid: “buying 10 cutlets at 250” — coins are frozen until the deal; withdraw the bid and they come back;
  • a cutlet costs no less than 200 🪙: fuel from the shop for Stars is always a better deal than the market; on the market it's bought by those who are short on time;
  • minimum prices for 1 item (for both sales and bids): ⚙️ parts and 💎 crystals — 10 🪙, ⛏ metal and 💧 hydrogen — 20, 🪨 stone — 30, ⚡ electricity — 50, 🪵 wood — 70, 🔥 fire — 170, 🔋 batteries — 300, 🐄 cow — 1,000, ☢️ Core — 5,000 🪙;
  • up to 100 cutlets or 1000 of a resource per order; how many orders you can hold at once depends on horses and carts: 2 by default (more in the “Stable” section);
  • the 10% fee is paid by the seller and burns for good — this protects coins from inflation;
  • resource swaps: “giving 60 metal for 25 hydrogen” — no coins and no fee; by minimum prices the two sides can differ by no more than 3 times;
  • while an account is new (below intelligence level 3 and 3 daily cutlets), you can buy at no more than three times the minimum price — so fake accounts can't pump coins around; you can withdraw your own resource lot if it fits in your storage.

Cutlets from the Fridge can't go to the market — only from storage.

🧰 Resources and the workshop: every game mines its own

Every win brings not only coins but also that game's resource:

  • ⛏ Metal — RPS;
  • 💧 Hydrogen — Higher-Lower;
  • 💎 Crystals — Quiz;
  • ⚙️ Parts — Cyber-Safe (3 per crack — it opens less often);
  • 🗺 first completion of a quest — 20–50 🪙 and 🪨 1–2 stone, of an epic — 100 🪙 and 🪨 5.

New raw materials — for building, logistics and the Lab:

  • 🪨 Stone — quests, streaks of correct quiz answers, mines, the chest;
  • 🪵 Wood — mines, the chest;
  • 🔥 Fire — the Bonfire in the workshop: wood + stone. Without fire there are no tools and no batteries;
  • ⚡ Electricity — a 25% chance for any win in a solo game (in the Electrician suit — 50–70%), every 10 correct answers in Math, the chest;
  • 🔋 Batteries — the workshop (electricity + metal + fire). A valuable resource: Shuttle charge and a team's entry for a mine battle;
  • 🐄 Cows — the chest on every 7th day of a streak, the market. One cow makes 3 cutlets in the workshop;
  • ☢️ Core — the rarest: only organizer prizes for battles, expeditions and events. Sold on the market from 5,000 🪙 (it can't be swapped for other resources).

The shop doesn't sell resources for Stars — they're only mined. Players trade them with each other on the market (for coins, the 10% fee burns) and swap resource for resource. They're used in the workshop — the “🔧 Workshop” tab in the game app:

  • ❄️ Nitrogen Cooling for 6 h, 🔋 Nuclear Boost for 3 h, 🛸 Master Drone for a day — the same boosts as in the shop, but short and paid for with resources;
  • 🛡 Raid shield for a day;
  • 🥩 cyber-cutlets from a cow — 🐄 a cow and 🔥 2 fire → 3 cutlets into storage (a cow on the market costs from 1,000 🪙; people buy it when they urgently need fuel for Nuclear Boost);
  • 🔥 Bonfire, 🔋 Batteries, 🛒 Cart (needs a horse), 💊 First-aid kit for 72 hours;
  • 🛡 Cyber-armor — +10% farm mining per level, up to 3 levels (+30%), each build lasts 30 days;
  • 🚀 Shuttle — 1000 of each resource + 1000 🪙, forever and only yours: a 5% fee instead of 10% on your sales, up to 500 cutlets per order and up to 16 open orders. The Shuttle runs on a battery: one 🔋 — 30 days of perks (charged in the “📦 Storage” tab).

Your stock is visible in the profile, in the workshop and in the Mini App passport. With Unlimited games, resources are mined with no daily cap — for every win.

📦 Storage with a limit. Storage holds 500 of each resource — anything mined beyond that is lost, so it pays to spend resources or sell them on the market. Storage is expanded in the workshop for good: 1000 → 2000 → 3000 → 4000 → 5000 of each resource; each expansion costs 20% more than the last and needs one more kind of resource. Storage holds finished items up to 10% of capacity in total: 500 → 50 items of all kinds together, 5000 → 500. Cutlets and coins aren't limited by storage.

🔥 Recipes of the week. From time to time the workshop gets temporary recipes: a shield for a week, a cutlet for 100 metal, smelting one resource into another, combo sets. Each has a build limit per week, and the recipes themselves vanish as suddenly as they appeared. Keep an eye on the “🔧 Workshop” tab.

📦 Items wait in storage. Hours of Nitrogen, Nuclear Boost, Master Drone, shields and Unlimited samples built in the app don't switch on right away — they go to the “📦 Storage” tab. Turn them on with a button whenever it suits you: a shield before bed, Nitrogen before a long break. The same tab has your resources, cutlets, the Shuttle, Cyber-armor and storage expansion. Armor, storage and the Shuttle take effect right after building.

🧪 New items:

  • 🎭 Camouflage (12 h) — you don't show up in raid scouting, but you can still attack;
  • 🪤 Decoy (24 h) — a raider who fails against you gives up twice as much;
  • 🎯 Stance trainer (24 h) — if a raid on you ends in a draw, it counts as the raider's failure;
  • 🔥 Ship afterburner — instantly resets the raid cooldown (no more than once a day);
  • ⛽ Fuel saver — the next launch needs no hydrogen;
  • 📦 Extended cargo hold — loot from the next raid ×1.5;
  • 🧊 Cutlet thermos (24 h, once a week) — Drillers eat cutlets 25% slower;
  • 📜 Market stamp (once a week) — your next market sale has no fee;
  • 🏳️ Team banner (24 h) — +5% mining for your whole team;
  • 🛡 Team dome (12 h) — a raid shield for the whole team.

Team items are expensive and complex (they need fire, batteries and the ☢️ Core), and only a player on a team can build them — teammates pool resources. A Mega-Clan protects up to 1000 fighters, so it pays 5 times more than a team for the Banner and the Dome. Complex builds need a tool kit from the Lab.

One-time items (Fuel saver, Cargo hold, Stamp) get “charged” in storage and kick in on the next matching action. For newcomers, workshop recipes unlock one per day — from the simplest and most useful to the legendary; locked ones are shown greyed out with the time until they open.

🏴‍☠️ Raids: decision against decision

The “🏴‍☠️ Raids” tab in the game app. Raids need the Lab technologies 📡 Comms, 🔭 Scouting and ⚔️ Attack at level 1. Scouting is free and shows 2 + your Scouting level targets; raid fuel is 5 💧 hydrogen per launch. You pick a gesture (rock, paper, scissors), and the target has a defense stance set in advance — the outcome is decided by RPS: your gesture against their stance. Win: you take 20% of each of the target's resources (no more than 30 per resource). Fail: you give the target 10% of yours (no more than 15). Draw — the fuel is burned.

  • only resources can be looted: coins, robots, cutlets and market orders are untouchable;
  • newcomers are protected for the first 3 days, teammates can't be raided, the same target — no more than once every 12 h;
  • one launch every 3 hours; the victim gets a message and has a free rematch for 24 hours (there's no rematch on a rematch — otherwise a pair of players could pump resources back and forth forever);
  • 🛡 a shield from the workshop keeps you safe from raids: build it, turn it on from storage, and the shield panel on the raids tab shows a timer until the protection ends;
  • change your stance in the raids menu — predictable players get raided more often;
  • ⚔️ each Attack level after the first — +8% to the spoils, 🛡 each Defense level of the target — −8%, 📚 Science — +1% per level; 📡 Comms cuts the cooldown by 20 minutes per level;
  • 💊 without a First-aid kit, 15% of a raid's spoils are “lost in the infirmary” (the target still loses all of it).

Targets are shown by name, without @username or ID — raids stay a game, not an excuse for bullying.

🎯 Daily goals and the chest

The “🎯 Goals” card in the game app's passport: every day you have three goals — from games, duels, the farm, the workshop, raids, the market and the team station. Complete all three and grab the chest: +50 🪙 and +10 of a random resource, plus a special bonus of the day: 🪵 wood, 🪨 stone or ⚡ electricity. Your day streak builds up: every 7th day in a row the chest adds +200 🪙 and a 🐄 cow. Goals refresh at 00:00 UTC.

🔬 Lab: tools and technologies

The “🔬 Lab” tab in the game app. Tool kits come in 4 levels: ⚒️ Rough tools, 🛠 Fine-tuning tools, 🔬 Calibration, 🧬 Nanotech. A kit is never used up — it unlocks complex builds in the workshop and the next technology levels.

  • 📡 Comms — needed for raids and mine income, then cuts the raid cooldown by 20 minutes per level;
  • 🔭 Scouting — +1 target in scouting per level;
  • ⚔️ Attack — no raids without it; then +8% to the spoils per level;
  • 🛡 Defense — a raider carries off 8% less from you per level;
  • 📚 Science — +1% to farm mining and spoils per level; needs the ☢️ Core.

Each technology has 5 levels. Level 1 of Comms, Scouting, Attack and Defense can be researched from day one — if you have the right tool kit and resources. Each next level of the same technology comes 30 days after the previous one (in the Scientist suit — 20–40% faster): every upgrade is something to look forward to and worth a lot. Research is never reset.

Science grows after the others: its level 1 opens when all four technologies are researched to level 1, level 2 — when all of them reach level 2, and so on.

🐎 Stable, carts and market lots

How many orders you can keep on the market at once depends on logistics: 2 lots by default, each 🐎 horse gives +1, each 🛒 cart another +1 (no more carts than horses; 5 of each at most). The game itself sells horses for coins (1500 / 3000 / 6000 / 10,000 / 15,000 🪙 — the coins burn), carts are built in the workshop. A charged Shuttle has its own limit — 16 lots. The stable is in the “📦 Storage” tab.

🏆 Seasons: TOP of the month

A season is a calendar month (12 seasons a year). Coins, robots, storage and purchases are not reset — only the tables start over: the TOP of players by coins earned in the game during the month (transfers and gifts don't count), the team ranking by places in the season's battles and the friends ranking in the “🤝 Friends” tab. On the 1st, a veteran and a newcomer start on equal footing.

The TOP-10 get a season badge in their passport forever — a medal for the place and the season's month, for example “🥈 2026-11” (places 4–10 — 🏅). Prizes for the winners are set by the organizer — the bot will message you privately with what was credited.

The “🏆 Season | 🎯 Events | 💰 Richest” switch is in the TOP tab.

🛰 Team station: a shared project

The “🏆 Team” tab in the game app. The whole team builds the station: any member donates resources, and a level is complete when 300 × the level of each resource has been donated (5 levels in total). Each level gives every fighter on the team +2% farm mining (up to +10%). Leave the team — lose the bonus. The feed of recent donations shows who's pulling the common cause.

⛏ Mines: the map and battles for resources

The “⛏ Mines” tab in the game app is a map of 30 mines: metal, hydrogen, crystals, parts, stone and wood — 5 of each. Mines are only for teams and clans. In a mine battle, places go to the teams with the best combined result of all fighters in the battle's nomination; only those who submitted a full battery entry are counted. While there's no battle, a mine raid is impossible — the map just shows who owns what.

  • the winner holds the mine for 24 hours — so every team gets a chance to play;
  • every fighter on the team gets 1 of the mine's resource per hour (within their own storage) — up to 24 units per run; a team of 50 fighters pumps out 1200;
  • places and their rewards are set by the organizer, who may also give the other participants a participation reward;
  • after that the mine is free, but it can be captured again only in a new battle;
  • mining goes to fighters with 📡 Comms level 1; without a 💊 First-aid kit every 7th hour is “sick”, with no mining;
  • the tab shows how much has already been mined and how long is left until the hold ends.

More fighters on the team means more loot. Invite friends and nudge your teammates during the battle!

🤝 Team market and mutual help

Team market. In the “📈 Market” tab switch to “Team market”: these orders and swaps can be seen and filled only by your teammates, and the fee is lower than on the common market — 5% instead of 10%, and for a Mega-Clan just 2%. The cutlet price floor (200 🪙) applies here too. Leave the team — your team lots are withdrawn, and the items and coins come back.

Mutual help. In the “🏆 Team” tab ask for a resource (one open request, up to 200 units), and your teammates will gift it. Players can gift from intelligence level 3 and after 3 daily cutlets, no more than 100 resources a day and no more than fits in the requester's storage. Cutlets can't be gifted — they're fuel from the shop.

🎖 Ranks for time in the ecosystem

The longer you're in Cyber Barons, the cooler the rank to the right of your name — in your profile, in the teammates list and in the Mini App TOP-100. Ranks go from the lowest to the coolest:

  • 🎖 Rookie — the first 72 hours after sign-up: nobody can raid you, so you can settle in calmly;
  • 🎖 Private — the next 7 days;
  • 🎖 Fighter — the next 15 days;
